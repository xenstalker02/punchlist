# Code of Conduct

Punchlist is a working repository for people discussing interface failures, evidence, and evaluator methods. Treat other participants as collaborators, even when the review is sharp.

## Expected conduct

Be specific about the work. Challenge a claim with evidence, ask for clarification when context is missing, and make room for people with different disciplines and experience. Do not use slurs, threats, harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or public disclosure of someone else's private information.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that conflict with this standard. To report a conduct concern privately, use the repository's [security contact](SECURITY.md). Include only the information needed to understand what happened; do not publish private details in an issue.
